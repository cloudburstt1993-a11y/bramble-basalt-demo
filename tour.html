Demo guide · Built by Bookcliff Web

Everything a local business website can do, in one demo.

Bramble & Basalt Garden Co. is a made-up business: a garden center, landscaping crew and café. That mix lets one site show 55 pages and 87 features a roofer, clinic, restaurant, salon, contractor or shop might want.

Start the tour with labels on Jump to the feature list
  1. 1

    Flip on “Feature labels” in the dark bar at the top. Every feature gets a yellow tag.

  2. 2

    Use it like a customer. Add to cart, book a time, sign an estimate, order a latte. Every flow finishes.

  3. 3

    Watch the tracking. With labels on, every call tap, form and sale pops up the GA4 event it sends.

Turn visitors into calls & leads

  • Tap-to-call everywhereEvery phone number dials on a phone, and every tap is counted.
  • Sticky call · text · book barAlways under the thumb on mobile, on every page.
  • Call to action above the foldBook or call without scrolling, on any screen.
  • Chat assistantAnswers hours, prices and booking questions, then takes a callback number.
  • Call tracking by adVisitors from Google Ads see a tracking number for their whole visit, so you know which ads ring the phone.
  • Lead source on every formEach enquiry arrives tagged with the ad, page or flyer that brought it in.
  • Contact form with real confirmationSpam-filtered, emailed instantly, tells the customer what happens next.
  • Instant price calculatorVisitors price their own project, then ask for a fixed quote.
  • Multi-step quote form + photo uploadFewer abandoned forms, better-qualified leads, fewer wasted trips.
  • Promo landing pageNo menu, one goal. For ads, mailers and QR codes.
  • Thank-you pageWhere forms land, so ad platforms count the conversion.
  • Storm & urgent request pagePhotos, a safety check and a direct line for time-sensitive jobs.
  • Link-in-bio pageOne tracked link for Instagram, TikTok and Facebook profiles.
  • Coming-soon pageCountdown to a real opening date and an email list for launch day.
  • Announcement barPromote what matters this week across the whole site.
  • “Do you serve my area?” checkerAnswers the first question before anyone has to call.

Sell online

  • Online storeSearch, category filters, sorting and an in-stock toggle.
  • Product pages with optionsSizes and prices, image gallery, specs, care info.
  • Cart: pickup, delivery or shippingKnows live plants can't ship and offers the right options.
  • Promo codesTry FALL10 or BLOOM5 in the cart.
  • Stripe checkout, Apple Pay & Google PaySecure hosted payment. Card numbers never touch the website.
  • Product finder quizFour questions, then the right products with add-to-cart.
  • Saved items & shareable listsHeart anything, share the list, add it all to the cart later.
  • Recently viewedBrings back what a shopper looked at last time.
  • Back-in-stock alertsOut-of-stock pages collect emails instead of losing the sale.
  • Digital gift cardsLive preview, custom message, scheduled delivery, balance check.
  • Coupons pageCopy a code, show it on a phone, or print the page.
  • Equipment rentalsDate-range pricing, availability check and a refundable deposit.
  • Catering order builderHeadcount pricing, add-ons and a deposit, for restaurants and caterers.
  • Trade / wholesale accountsB2B applications with tax-exempt upload and net-30 terms.
  • Sale prices & badgesStrike-through pricing, New, Native and Sale tags.
  • Shoppable articlesBlog posts that sell the products they mention.

Bookings, signatures & payments

  • Online bookingServices, live time slots, closed days, confirmation.
  • Book a specific personCustomers can request a staff member, or take the first opening.
  • Add to calendarApple, Outlook and Google calendar files after booking.
  • Estimate approval + e-signatureClients pick options, sign on their phone and pay the deposit.
  • Online waiversSigned before arrival, with extra fields for minors.
  • Events with tickets & RSVPsClasses, free events and paid tickets in one calendar.
  • Single event pages + countdownShareable page per event with Google event data.
  • Subscription plansMonthly or prepaid billing, comparison table.
  • Loyalty program signupPoints, rewards and birthday perks.
  • Client portalVisits, photos, invoices, documents and billing in one place.
  • Pay an invoice onlineGet paid faster with no paper checks.
  • Financing & pay laterMonthly payment calculator, plus Affirm and Klarna at checkout.
  • DonationsAny amount, one-time or monthly, for causes and nonprofits.
  • Restaurant menu with diet filtersVegetarian, vegan and gluten-free at a tap.
  • Order ahead for pickupPickup times that follow the real opening hours.
  • Venue & private event inquiriesTurns the space itself into a revenue stream.

Build trust

  • Reviews pageRating summary, filters by service and owner replies.
  • Video testimonialsLoad only when tapped, so the page stays fast.
  • Google reviews & rating badgeReal reviews on a live site, plus a “leave a review” link for everyone.
  • Project case studyChallenge, solution, results, materials and budget range.
  • Before / after slidersDrag to compare. Works with touch and keyboard.
  • Project gallery with lightboxFilter by type, swipe through full-size photos.
  • Team member pagesBio, specialties, work and a “book with me” button.
  • Our story, timeline & badgesFaces and history make a small business feel safe to hire.
  • Published price listStarting prices for everything, with pricing promises.
  • Process timeline & packagesSets expectations before the first call.
  • Community impact pageLocal giving with numbers and a sponsorship form.
  • Referral programPersonal share links. Friends see the offer when they arrive.
  • Live “open now” hoursShows open or closed in local time, automatically.
  • Product reviewsStar ratings and a “write a review” form.

Get found on Google

  • Dedicated service pagesOne page per service, the way Google likes it.
  • City landing pagesLocal projects, reviews and answers for each town served.
  • Multiple locations + nearest finderGPS or ZIP code picks the closest store.
  • Spanish-language pageTells Google which language each version is for.
  • Job postings for Google JobsEach opening can show up in Google's job search.
  • Structured dataBusiness, FAQ, article, event, job and breadcrumb markup.
  • Blog with categoriesMonthly articles, author bios, table of contents, share buttons.
  • Searchable FAQType a word, see only matching answers.
  • Site searchFinds pages, products, articles and answers as you type.
  • Site mapEvery page in one list, kept up to date automatically.
  • Branded link previewsA clean card with image when a link is texted or posted.
  • Consistent name, address, phoneMatches the Google Business Profile on every page.
  • Breadcrumbs & jump linksEasy to navigate for people and search engines.

Run the business

  • Conversion tracking (GA4)Calls, texts, forms, bookings and sales each send an event.
  • QR codes with campaign tagsCount the leads each flyer, sign or truck wrap brings in.
  • Free guide for an emailA PDF lead magnet that grows the customer list.
  • Printable guides & pagesClean printouts and PDFs from any page.
  • Smart formsQuestions appear only when they apply.
  • Careers + resume uploadJob listings and an application form that takes files.
  • Live weather tipLive National Weather Service data turned into today's advice.
  • Works offline, installs like an appPages stay usable with no signal and can sit on a home screen.
  • Cookie consent bannerTap to preview. Analytics stays off until a visitor allows it.
  • Newsletter signupGrow a customer list on every page.
  • Friendly 404 pageBroken links still lead somewhere useful.
  • Privacy, terms & accessibilityThe pages every business needs before launch.
