Privacy policy

We collect only what we need to answer you, book your visit or fill your order: your name, contact details, address for on-site work, and what you tell us about your project.

  • Forms are delivered to our inbox through Formspree. We don't sell or share your details.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Card numbers go to Stripe directly and never touch this website or our staff.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to see which pages are useful and how people contact us. It doesn't identify you personally.
  • Texts. We only text about your own appointments and orders. Reply STOP anytime.

Want your information deleted? Email hello@brambleandbasalt.example and we'll confirm within a week.

Terms of sale

  • Unused goods can be returned within 30 days with a receipt.
  • Plants carry a 1-year guarantee when planted per our care card; installed plants carry a 2-year warranty.
  • Landscape quotes are fixed for 30 days. Deposits are refundable until materials are ordered.
  • Workshop tickets are refundable up to 48 hours before the class.
  • Gift cards never expire and can't be redeemed for cash except where required by law.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It's built to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA: every page works with a keyboard alone, text meets contrast guidelines, images have descriptions, and forms have clear labels and error messages.

If anything gets in your way, call (970) 555-0142 and we'll help you directly, and fix the page.
