Questions, answered

Search below, or text a photo of your yard to (970) 555-0142.

Landscaping & estimates

Are your estimates really free?
Yes. On-site estimates for landscaping, hardscape and irrigation are free and come with a written, line-item quote within three business days. Design consultations are $95 and credited to your project.
How far out are you booking?
Estimates usually within a week. Install projects are typically 3–6 weeks out in spring and 1–3 weeks in fall. The booking page shows live availability.
What does the 2-year warranty cover?
Any plant we installed that fails within two years is replaced free, as long as the irrigation we set up stays on. Hardscape workmanship is covered for two years too.
Do you offer financing?
Projects over $2,500 can be paid monthly through our lending partner, subject to approval. Try the payment calculator.

Online orders & pickup

How fast is online order pickup?
Most orders are ready in about two hours during open hours. We text you when it's ready; pull into the pickup spots by the café.
Do you deliver?
Yes, around the Grand Valley for $25, free over $150. Bulk mulch and soil have a two-yard minimum. Tools, seed and pottery also ship anywhere in the US.
Can live plants be shipped?
No. Plants are pickup or local delivery only, so they arrive healthy.
What's your return policy?
Unused goods can be returned within 30 days with a receipt. Plants carry a 1-year guarantee when planted per our care card.

Yard care plans

Can I pause my yard care plan?
Yes. Text us the dates and we'll skip those visits and credit your next bill.
How does billing work?
Plans bill monthly by card or bank account through Stripe, April through November. Prepay the season and save 10%.

Café, events & gift cards

Do I need to order ahead at the café?
No, walk-ins are always welcome. Ordering ahead just means your coffee is waiting on the shelf.
Can I get a refund on a workshop ticket?
Full refund up to 48 hours before the class, or transfer your seat to a friend anytime.
Do gift cards expire?
Never. They work at the garden center, café, workshops and on landscaping invoices.
