Landscape crew member

  • $19–$24/hr
  • Full-time
  • Grand Junction
  • Hiring 4
Apply in 5 minutes

You'll be on a three-person crew building the yards in our gallery: setting flagstone, running drip lines, planting natives and leaving every site cleaner than you found it.

What you'll do

  • Install plants, irrigation, edging and stone on residential projects
  • Run mowers, trimmers and a skid steer (we'll train and certify you)
  • Keep job sites tidy and text the client progress photos

What you need

  • A valid driver's license
  • Ability to lift 50 lb and work outdoors all day
  • A good attitude. Experience is a bonus, not a requirement

Schedule

Monday to Friday, 7 am to 3:30 pm, with optional Saturday overtime in spring. Winter work on the snow crew and in the greenhouse keeps the paychecks coming.

Benefits

  • Paid every Friday
  • Paid training & certifications
  • 1 week paid time off to start
  • 30% off plants, free coffee
