Case study · Redlands

From dust bowl to backyard dinners in 11 days

A bare dirt lot with a broken fence became a flagstone patio, a cedar pergola and a native garden that sips water.

The challenge

The Garcias bought a new build with a 1,400 sq ft backyard of compacted clay, construction debris and a fence the wind had already broken. Their wish list: somewhere to eat dinner outside, shade in July, and nothing that needed a sprinkler.

What we built

  • A 320 sq ft flagstone patio on a compacted base, sized for a table of six
  • A 12×14 cedar pergola with string lights
  • 32 native plants, 3 boulders and shredded cedar mulch on two drip zones
  • A rebuilt fence section and a gravel side path to the gate

The result

The yard uses roughly 60% less water than the sod the builder quoted, and the pergola shades the table from 3 pm on. “We eat out there every night it isn't snowing,” Maria told us at the walkthrough. Sample quote
