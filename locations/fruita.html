Fruita, Colorado

Landscaping in Fruita, from the monument to the river.

We've built more than 80 yards in Fruita. Our materials yard is on the west side of town, so there's no travel fee and bulk deliveries arrive same day.

Free Fruita estimate (970) 555-0142

What Fruita yards need

Wind off the Book Cliffs, alkaline clay and long, hot afternoons. We plan for all three.

  • Plants that take the heatNatives like Apache plume and blue grama that thrive at 4,500 feet.
  • Drip that beats the windSpray heads lose half their water on a breezy Fruita afternoon. Drip doesn't.
  • Soil prep for clayWe amend beds, not whole yards, so natives aren't overfed.
