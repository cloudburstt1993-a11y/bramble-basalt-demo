All FAQs
Are your estimates really free?
Yes. On-site estimates for landscaping, hardscape and irrigation are free and come with a written, line-item quote within three business days. Design consultations are $95 and credited to your project.
Can I order plants online and pick them up?
Yes. Most orders are ready in about two hours. Pull into the pickup spots by the café and we'll bring it out.
Do you offer financing?
Projects over $2,500 can be paid monthly through our lending partner, subject to approval. Use the payment calculator to see a rough monthly cost.
When should I book a sprinkler blowout?
Mid-October through mid-November, before the first hard freeze. Slots fill fast, so book online early.
