Our story

It started with a pickup truck full of junipers.

In 2009, Maya Ortega sold plants off a tailgate on the edge of town. Neighbors kept asking if she'd plant them too. Seventeen years later, we're a garden center, a landscaping crew of 22 and a café, still on the same corner.

What we believe

Three promises we keep

Plant for the place

This is high desert. We'll talk you out of a plant that won't make it, even if it costs us the sale.

Pick up the phone

A real person answers during business hours, and texts get a reply within the hour.

Price it in writing

Starting prices on the website, fixed quotes on paper. No surprise invoices, ever.
